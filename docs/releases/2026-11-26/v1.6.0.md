## Scope

`v1.6.0` is the RA + HA edge research platform: the distributed, single-operator
research deployment with a browser client, a command shell, and the seven
services on one image. The release closes the 46-leaf RA/HA plan, with 45
technical, QA, and documentation leaves done and DOC-004 carrying the release
record.

Included:

- the RA pack, signal, lineage, research, and simulation surfaces, and the 61
  command gateway contract shared by the browser and the shell;
- the v3 HA profile: Patroni/etcd/HAProxy PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ quorum queues,
  MinIO erasure coding, Traefik ingress, observability, backup/PITR/DR, queue
  rebuild, object archive, and the lab matrix;
- the release supply chain: pinned image bases, SPDX SBOM, Grype plus
  `cargo audit`, the expiring CVE allowlist, the staging-to-version digest
  check, and the offline signature step;
- the v1.6.0 release-gate fixes: CVSS vector parsing, the unused AWS legacy TLS
  graph removal, the curl-free runtime image with the gateway health probe, the
  ten-container stack count, and the 1.6.0 version/oracle alignment.

Deliberately excluded:

- a claim of `Real HA`; the maximum is `Simulated HA` on the single-host lab,
  and HA-22/HA-23 remain operator-only `Unverified`;
- a 72-hour soak or a versioned upgrade; the recorded soak is a 21-minute
  sample and the cutover/rollback drill is separate;
- the manual image signature; no offline cosign key is available, so the
  release body carries `Image signature: pending`.

## Risks

- The 72 Debian trixie High advisories are unfixed in the stable suite; they are
  recorded in `deploy/release/cve-allowlist.json` with ticket #1131 and expire
  on 2027-02-28. No Critical is allowlisted.
- The image signature is pending; the operator must sign the published digest
  with the offline cosign key and record the bundle hash in the release body.
- `Real HA` is not claimed; HA-22/HA-23 and the three-host LAN run remain
  operator gates. The single-host lab is `Simulated HA`.
- The 72-hour soak and the versioned upgrade/rollback are not complete; the
  recorded soak is a 21-minute sample. They remain residual risks for this
  release.
- The SBOM and CVE hashes above are the snapshot evidence. The tag workflow
  regenerates both from the same pinned base and records the final values in the
  GitHub release.
