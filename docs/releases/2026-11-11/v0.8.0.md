## Scope

This is the last shell-only release before the Tauri Platform. It freezes the
current deterministic research shell and records the work accumulated since
`v0.7.0`.

Included:

- The Binance BTCUSDT public-archive importer, coverage audit, and admitted
  cross-venue source.
- `analytics evaluate` buy-and-hold and matched random-timing benchmarks, with
  explicit seed, path count, costs, and null reasons.
- The deterministic trade-chart tool, trade-context exporter, annotation mapper,
  exit-quality table, and click annotator undo.
- The `M1.3` model identity, registry `2026-11-10.2`, corrected pivot-sequence
  occurrence and Fibonacci/band tests, and the fixed tolerances.
- Strategy position excursions and the configurable giveback exit.
- The annotation-zone extractor and the 2018–2019 report.
- The raw-edge, event-edge, scale-screen, and fresh-onset breakout diagnostics.
- The simulation-run harness and `simulation-research` workflow.
- The persistent local data root and the mandatory worktree-cleanup guard.

Excluded:

- The Tauri desktop platform.
- The removal of the policy/prop-firm layer.
- Accounts, team cloud, sync, or subscription.
- Windows and macOS packaging.
- Validation partition access and any new model, strategy, or portfolio baseline.

## Risks

- The current workspace package version remains `0.3.0`; release identity is the
  annotated tag and this note. The Tauri Platform will align product versioning
  separately.
- No strategy or portfolio baseline is accepted. The SR1–SR13 and simulation
  records remain research evidence, not an edge claim.
- The local data tree is large and is not part of the release. Large research
  artifacts remain outside the repository or in the local data root.
- The policy/prop-firm layer is still present in this release; removing it is the
  first contract change of the Tauri Platform.

## Rollback

The previous release is `v0.7.0`. This release is additive: research tools,
model corrections, benchmarks, trade context, and cleanup tooling. It does not
change the S3 command contracts or the policy contract. A rollback is a checkout
of `v0.7.0`; no data migration is required.
