# v0.5.0 — Workflow Reset Baseline

- Date: 2026-11-01 (UTC)
- Tag: `v0.5.0`
- Commit: `42307b7` (the pre-reset `main` head)
- Status: Baseline tag; not a feature release

## Purpose

`v0.5.0` marks the last `main` head before the 2026-11-01 workflow reset. It is
the rollback point for the Audit Workflow and Improvement Workflow changes.

## What is in the tag

- The unified ladder and the v3/v4/v5 candidate paths as they existed at
  `42307b7`.
- The pre-reset experiment records under `docs/experiments/` and audits under
  `docs/audits/`.
- The pre-reset `data/labs`, `data/reports`, and `data/research` conventions.

## What changed after the tag

- The old CI-labelled loop became the **Audit Workflow** (audit, remediation
  plan, execution, verification) and the **Improvement Workflow** (run, results,
  review, new hypothesis).
- The improvement run store and the tiered model/strategy/portfolio contract
  were added.
- v6 `breakout` was added as an experimental candidate.
- Pre-reset evidence was invalidated in `docs/improvement/INVALIDATIONS.md`;
  physical cleanup is a later phase.

## Verification

The tag points at the exact commit `42307b7`. The post-tag gates are recorded in
the workflow-reset audit and its verification record.
