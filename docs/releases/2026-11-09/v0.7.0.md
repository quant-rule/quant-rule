## Scope

This release adds the model and data features used by the SR9 15m research
programme and records the programme’s no-candidate result.

Included:

- `vol_regime` and `structure_state` context states, strategy-readable and
  additive to existing event values.
- `signal.breakout_retest_hold`, event code 146, subtype 24, source 5.
- Optional session calendar in `data.json` and session-aware coverage fields;
  the span coverage is retained.
- SR9 configs, qualification criteria, campaign record, and the break-only BTC
  long control at 6 and 9 bps.
- `rvol_regime`, `M1.1` opening-range sweep/control, COPPER/TSLA price-only
  admission, and the research tooling and workflow documentation accumulated
  since `v0.6.0`.
- The SR9 data-config source-path fix.

Excluded:

- 2024 validation access.
- An accepted strategy or portfolio baseline.
- A promoted break-only control.
- Deferred local data cleanup.

## Risks

- No strategy or portfolio baseline is accepted. SR9 is underpowered and does
  not support an edge claim.
- The break-only BTC long control is positive at 6 and 9 bps but is not a
  candidate; it needs a new pre-registered programme and out-of-sample data.
- BTC 15m coverage is below 95% in 2018, 2019, and 2022; XAU 2019 and EUR 2021
  are slightly below 95%.
- 2024 remains closed.
- Local campaign artifacts are large (3.5 GB) and are not part of the release.

## Rollback

The previous release is `v0.6.0`. This release is additive: new model states, a
new event, optional data/report fields, configs, and records. Existing event
values, strategy decisions, and portfolio accounting are unchanged. A rollback
is a checkout of `v0.6.0`; the new states/event and the SR9 records are removed
with the release commit. No data migration is required.
