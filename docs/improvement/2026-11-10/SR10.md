- 10 of 19 exits are stops, with several entries one or two bars apart.

entry rule is too permissive: the 3-bar event window allows repeated entries in
the same cluster, and the giveback exit underperforms the structure control.

follow-up is a new hypothesis, `SR11`, with a same-bar entry window and trend
alignment. SR10 is not validation evidence for SR11.
