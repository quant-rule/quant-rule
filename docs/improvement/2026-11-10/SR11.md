## 1. Intent

Test whether a same-bar EMA/Fibonacci retest entry, aligned with `ema_stack` and
`structure_state`, removes SR10's countertrend clusters and produces a
cost-adjusted edge on Dukascopy BTCUSD 15m.

## 2. What ran

The full pre-registered matrix: four primary cells × 2018–2022 × 6 and 9 bps
(40 year-cells). All 40 completed with no run error.

| Cell | Side | Exit |
|---|---|---|
| SR11.1 | long | `structure_state = −1` |
| SR11.2 | short | `structure_state = +1` |
| SR11.3 | long | MACD histogram slope flip −1 |
| SR11.4 | short | MACD histogram slope flip +1 |

The entry is `level.fib_test` + `level.test` source 1, with `ema_stack` and
`structure_state` aligned, on one bar. All cells use a 1 ATR stop, 3 ATR target,
50-bar time stop, $10,000 fixed notional, 1x, one position, and a 5% hard halt.

The model was computed on the 15m ladder only (`model_timeframes: ["15m"]`); the
data source remains the same 1m Dukascopy series. The 15m rows of the optimized
bundle are byte-identical to the full-ladder SR10 2018 model (readings, events,
levels, and legs), so the restriction is behavior-neutral. Runtime fell from
about 8m47 to about 13–22s per year-cell.

## 4. Diagnosis

The designed 3:1 reward-to-risk did not survive the exits. Realized payoff ratios
are 2.31, 2.14, 1.61, and 0.98. At those ratios the breakeven win rates are about
30.2%, 31.8%, 38.3%, and 50.4%; observed win rates are 22.8%, 24.4%, 27.0%, and
31.5%. Every cell is below its own breakeven.

- The structure exit is the better base: payoff about 2.2 and targets reached in
  20–24% of trades.
- The force exit is clearly worse: it cuts winners (targets in 6–9% of trades)
  and collapses payoff to 1.61 and 0.98.
- SR11.4's 31.5% win rate is not enough because its average win barely exceeds
  its average loss.
- The 1 ATR stop is hit in most trades, and the 3 ATR target is rarely reached.
  The entry is a mean-reversion tap, but the exit geometry assumes a trend
  continuation.

The 5% hard halt is binding in every year-cell. That truncates the full-sample
result and makes the aggregate PF pessimistic at the halt point, but it does not
rescue the entry: the pre-halt win rate is below breakeven in every cell, and the
target rate is below breakeven in the structure cells.

## 5. Raw-edge diagnostic

Because the 5% halt truncated every year, the entry was measured without any
stop, target, halt, or position state: every signal, entry at the next bar open,
forward returns and MFE/MAE at 1–20 bars, and a 3 ATR target against a 1 ATR stop
over 20 bars. Full report: [`sr11-raw-edge.md`](../../reports/2026-11-10/sr11-raw-edge.md).

| Side | Signals | 20-bar net | 20-bar hit | Target | Stop | Mean R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Long | 2,338 | −0.0502% | 48.1% | 20.2% | 70.1% | −0.0075 |
| Short | 2,269 | −0.0188% | 47.0% | 20.1% | 69.4% | −0.0212 |

The non-overlapping 95% intervals include zero; the point estimates are
negative. Average 20-bar MFE is 2.72 ATR long and 2.84 ATR short, below the 3 ATR
target; average MAE is 2.91 and 2.49 ATR, far beyond the 1 ATR stop. Targets are
reached in about 20% of signals, below the 25% rate a 3:1 bracket needs when
timeout outcomes are ignored.

The full signal does not beat the event-only, states-only, or unconditional
controls on mean R. The state filter is not adding value, and no variant has a
positive 20-bar net return at 6 bps. The halt was not hiding an edge.

## 6. Decision

SR11 is falsified under its pre-registered criteria and is stopped. There is no
candidate, no baseline, and no validation access. SR11 is not re-registered with
a different threshold, stop, target, timeframe, or filter. 2024 remains closed.

The raw-edge diagnostic confirms the entry has no positive edge, so the
portfolio-tier 0.5x sizing diagnostic is not run: it would only scale a flat to
negative result. The EMA/Fibonacci line is stopped. The next hypothesis must
start from a different entry premise or a different confirmation, with its own
pre-registration.
