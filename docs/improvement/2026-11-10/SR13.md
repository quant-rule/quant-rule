## 1. Intent

Test whether the first confirmed close outside the accepted 5m fresh-onset
compression range predicts continuation in the break direction over 6 bars after
costs. This is a directional claim about the close, not a volatility-expansion
claim about the range.

## 2. What ran

Five BTCUSD 5m model bundles (2018–2022) with the onset event enabled, and the
`breakout_edge` screen on the development partition:

- primary: 3-bar breakout window, 6-bar horizon, pooled sides, 6 bps base and
  9 bps stress;
- descriptive: 1-bar and 6-bar windows, 1/3/12-bar horizons, long and short
  sides separately;
- 6,379 non-overlapping signals over 2018–2022.

floor and was not pre-registered.
