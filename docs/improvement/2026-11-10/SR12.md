# SR12 — Failed Break/Reclaim and Band Fade

- Date: 2026-11-10 (UTC)
- Status: **Completed — H1 engine-blocked; H2 no candidate; sizing diagnostic confirms no edge; 2024 closed**
- Charter: [`SR12-CHARTER.md`](../../governance/2026-11-10/SR12-CHARTER.md)
- Predecessor: [`SR11`](SR11.md) — stopped; no raw edge
- Tier: strategy
- Model: `M1.2` development-only exception
- Claim type: strategy research; no edge claim.

## 1. Intent

Test two mechanically different premises after the EMA/Fibonacci line failed:

- **H1 — failed break and reclaim** at a confirmed level.
- **H2 — band fade** at a multi-touch horizontal band with a time exit.

Both were pre-registered together with a shared control, eight primary cells, and
a Bonferroni correction.

## 2. H1 — blocked before the matrix

Two engine constraints prevent the H1 rule as specified:

1. The compiled strategy registry exposes only each event's primary source.
   `level.break`/`level.cross` at major pivots (source 8) are not registered for
   the event-composite engine, even though the model publishes them.
2. The event-composite engine rejects entry events that disagree on direction
   (`entry events disagree on direction`). A break −1 plus a reclaim +1 is
   rejected at configuration time; `contrarian` does not change that check.

The rule was therefore corrected to the VWAP level (source 19), which is
registered, and screened as a diagnostic. It cannot be run as a strategy without
a sequence-aware entry condition or a strategy-side source allow-list — a
Requirements & Design change, not a strategy config.

H1 raw-edge screen at VWAP, 6 bps, 2:1 barrier over 20 bars:

| Cell | Signals | 20-bar net | Hit | Target | Mean R | All-bars mean R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H1 long | 4,125 | −0.0476% | 49.6% | 31.9% | +0.0201 | +0.0037 |
| H1 short | 4,268 | −0.0789% | 47.7% | 33.2% | +0.0316 | +0.0132 |

Controls: break-only long 62,461 signals, mean R +0.0182; cross-only long 9,115,
mean R +0.0381; break-only short 68,494, mean R +0.0219; cross-only short 9,113,
mean R +0.0293. The full conjunction is not better than the cross-only control,
and every 20-bar net return is negative after costs. H1 is stopped; no engine
change is justified.

## 5. Sizing diagnostic

H2.3 long 12 was the closest cell, so it was re-run at 0.5x and 0.2x notional,
with the commission held at the standard rate and with the commission scaled to
keep a 6 bps effective round trip. Charter:
[`SR12-SIZING.md`](../../governance/2026-11-10/SR12-SIZING.md).

| Variant | Trades | Net | PF | Max DD | Halted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1x 6 bps | 240 | +$506.14 | 1.072 | 5.44% | 5/5 |
| 1x 9 bps | 189 | +$128.53 | 1.022 | 5.42% | 5/5 |
| 0.5x same cost (9 bps) | 333 | −$1,080.88 | 0.808 | 5.29% | 5/5 |
| 0.2x same cost (18 bps) | 709 | −$2,122.00 | 0.637 | 5.14% | 5/5 |
| 0.5x 6 bps effective | 392 | −$956.53 | 0.843 | 5.35% | 5/5 |
| 0.2x 6 bps effective | 1,984 | −$1,616.23 | 0.866 | 6.13% | 4/5 |

Smaller size does not rescue the strategy. It removes the halt in some years and
lets the full sample express, but the full-sample PF is 0.81–0.87 at a 6 bps
effective cost, below 1.0. The 1x PF 1.072 was a truncated-sample artifact: the
5% halt stopped trading at drawdown lows and flattered the result. The same-cost
variants are worse still, because the equity-based commission raises the effective
cost to 9 bps at 0.5x and 18 bps at 0.2x.

The $1 / 0.1% notional idea is not viable: it is unexecutable at the quantity
step and the commission is about 30% of the position round trip. The 0.2x 6 bps
variant is the closest test to a 0.1% risk budget and it is negative.

## 6. Decision

No candidate. SR12 is stopped.

- H1 is engine-blocked; its VWAP screen has no positive net edge, so no engine
  change is proposed.
- H2 fails the pre-registered PF, drawdown, and no-halt criteria. The long
  12-bar cell is descriptive, not a candidate; it is not promoted or retuned.
- The sizing diagnostic confirms the full-sample result is negative; smaller
  size only changes how quickly the loss is realized.
- 2024 remains closed. No baseline and no edge claim.

## 8. Limitations

- One instrument (Dukascopy BTCUSD 15m); development partition only.
- H1 was not run as a strategy; its screen is descriptive.
- The 5% halt truncates every H2 year-cell; the aggregate PF is not a
  full-sample estimate.
- The H1 window does not enforce break-before-reclaim order; the engine has no
  sequence condition.
- The model-baseline exception remains development-only.
