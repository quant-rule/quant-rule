# Program PROGRAM-2026-11-04-03 — Early Progression Target

- Date: 2026-11-04 (UTC)
- Status: **Inconclusive — underpowered**
- Tier: `model`
- Validation: not opened
- Strategy: not opened
- Baseline: none

## 1. Premise

A confirmed progression leg in stage 0 or 1, with a stable published target,
reaches that target before a 1 ATR stop within 20 15m bars more often than a
matched target-distance base, and adds beyond the stage base.

The target is the model's published target price, not a fixed 2 ATR. The stop is
1 ATR from the entry.

## 2. Pre-flight sample

The gate's minimum is `n >= 200` per cell. The early-stage entry is the leg's
`origin_available_at + 3`, where the 15m reading stage is 0 or 1 and the leg
direction agrees with `progression_leg_direction`. The target must be stable
(`target_stable_code = 1`) and at its first version (`target_version = 0`).

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BTCUSD 2018 | 0 | 12 | 12 |
| BTCUSD 2019 | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| BTCUSD 2020 | 4 | 10 | 14 |
| ETHUSD 2018 | 3 | 20 | 23 |
| ETHUSD 2019 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| ETHUSD 2020 | 3 | 11 | 14 |
| XAUUSD 2018 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| XAUUSD 2019 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| XAUUSD 2020 | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| **Total** | **20** | **58** | **78** |

The stage-0 cell has 20 events; the stage-1 cell has 58. Combined, 78. Both are
far below the pre-registered minimum of 200.

The target-distance sample for BTCUSD 2018 has a median of 1.97 ATR and a mean of
2.83 ATR: the payoff is not the blocker. The sample is.

## 3. Decision

- The gate was **not opened**. The stage-0 and stage-1 cells cannot meet
  `n >= 200` on `model_dev`.
- No model validation, strategy run, or baseline was opened.
- The progression early-stage premise is recorded as **inconclusive —
  underpowered**, not as evidence that progression has no edge.
- Do not broaden the stage to 0–2 or switch to 5m/1m after seeing the sample.
  Stage 2 is a different premise with a smaller remaining target; the 5m/1m
  early-stage counts are lower (3 and 6 per BTCUSD year in the same check).
  A new progression hypothesis requires a new charter.
- The accepted compression model baselines are unchanged.
