## Intent and boundary

E2 turns the E1 execution vocabulary into a causal fill simulator. It consumes
one closed candle and the strategy decision made on that candle, fills a market
order scheduled by the previous candle at the next open, and manages one
position's ATR stop and target. It records fills and rejections only.

The module creates no portfolio accounting: cash, equity, trade records, and
P&L belong to P1/P2. It creates no command and performs no I/O. The shell
`backtest` command is B3; E2 exports the engine that path will drive. Model
Signal events remain a separate event type. No crate dependency or Cargo
manifest changed.

## Decisions and learning

- **The bracket uses the decision bar's ATR.** The entry fills at the next open,
  but the stop and target are sized by the ATR(14) that was knowable when the
  decision was made. Reading the fill bar's ATR would leak a value the strategy
  did not have.
- **The bracket is anchored at the entry fill price.** Slippage moves the entry,
  so the stop and target move with it; anchoring at the unslipped reference
  would make the realized risk differ from the recorded bracket.
- **A zero-volume bar is not tradable.** It rejects a pending market order and
  cannot trigger a bracket, while an absent volume is treated as tradable. The
  two cases are different: absent data is not evidence of no trading.
- **An exit at the open wins over a same-bar stop.** The market order fills
  first, so the position is flat before the bracket is evaluated.
- **`finish` does not close an open position.** The first backtest has no
  end-of-data exit; inventing one would add a fill the strategy never decided.
- **The engine rejects slippage at or above 100%.** A sell would otherwise fill
  at a non-positive price. E1's config still permits the value because it is a
  cost record, not a fill simulation.
- **The exit decision's side is ignored.** The engine closes the position it
  holds; the decision's side is the strategy's view, not the order side. An
  earlier draft returned an `ExitSideMismatch` error when the two differed; that
  check was removed because the engine already knows which position to close.
- **Wrong-timeframe filtering belongs to the caller.** The engine consumes one
  candle series and does not inspect a timeframe; the runner selects the
  strategy's timeframe, and the strategy already ignores other timeframes. The
  decision is still checked against the candle's bar index and open time.
- **Corrected during verification:** the missing-ATR test first supplied the
  invalid ATR on the fill bar instead of the decision bar, so the engine
  correctly used the earlier valid value and the test failed. The test was
  corrected, not the engine.
- **Rejected:** an end-of-data market exit, intrabar M1 fills, partial fills,
  order-book simulation, and portfolio fields on `Fill`. Each belongs to a later
  work item or is outside the approved scope.
