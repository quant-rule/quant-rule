## Entry: read-only review and corrections

- **Date and tool:** 2026-10-24 (UTC). A separate read-only review agent (same model family, independent session) reviewed the diff against the contract and the plan. It did not modify files or run git write commands.
- **Intent and boundary:** Check the formulas, availability rules, role registry, and test coverage. The review recomputed the checked-in fixture independently from its candles and confirmed the numeric readings.
- **Findings and corrections:**
  - Pivot candidates were absent from the registry, and the Progression origin use was attributed to confirmed pivots. Fixed by keying the pivot family with `PivotArtifact::Candidate` and `PivotArtifact::Confirmed` and recording both artifacts.
  - EMA had no Signal role for an EMA/EMA series crossover. Added for EMA20, EMA50, and EMA200.
  - ATR had no Divergence role for `price_delta_atr` and no Context role for `atr_ratio`. Added both.
  - The module documentation claimed the module knew nothing about axes or events while the registry defines their codes. Corrected to say the module computes no axis or event value.
  - The registry restated axis formulas, risking a second source of truth. Reworded the definitions to name the use and the contract section; the reading and axis modules own the arithmetic.
- **Test gaps closed:** an exact `(reading, artifact, target)` registry fixture; a changed-later-bar causality test over the golden series; a property test over input lengths 0..=40; a mixed-equality pivot test; a direct assertion that a pivot price equals the candle high or low at its occurrence bar; a fixture-level assertion that `available_at == occurred_at + lookback`; and a real slice-offset determinism check replacing the double reverse.
- **Verification and evidence:** after the corrections, `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`, and `RUSTDOCFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo doc --no-deps` are clean. `cargo test` reports 360 passed, 0 failed: data 90, model unit 100, model golden 11, shell unit 93, shell CLI 47, doc 19.
- **Learning:** The contract's §14 source codes cover confirmed pivots but not candidates, even though §8.4 calls the candidate a reading in its own right. An exact registry fixture is the only test that catches a missing use; a per-reading "at least one" check cannot. The changed-later-bar test is stronger than appending bars, because it can expose a hidden forward reference rather than only a length-dependent one.
