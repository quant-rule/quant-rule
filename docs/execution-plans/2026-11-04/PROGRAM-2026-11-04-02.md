## 1. Intent

Test whether a 15m directional event has a higher 2 ATR barrier hit rate when it
agrees with the last closed 1h trend state, and whether the 15m event adds
beyond the 1h state base.

This is the cross-timeframe condition the previous single-timeframe tests did
not include. The 15m event is a timing trigger inside the 1h trend, not a second
vote.

## 4. Decision

- The cross-timeframe premise is retired.
- The 1h trend filter improved the 15m trend event by only 1.13pp, and the
  back-lag control remained +16.6pp: the event is late even inside the 1h trend.
- The pivot break improved by 1.70pp but failed the lower bound, incremental,
  random, and back-lag checks.
- The EMA50 test was negative after the 1h filter.
- No baseline is accepted. The accepted compression model baselines are
  unchanged and direction-neutral.
- No variant with a different event, target, stop, horizon, timeframe, filter,
  or cost is permitted under this program.
